⛵ Welcome to the DBSC Race Course app

A quick tour — about 30 seconds.

1. Pick your course

  • Choose your Course card, the posted wind direction, and the Course # from the committee boat board.
  • The chart and leg list update instantly. Tapping a leg jumps to it.

2. Refine the wind

  • The course card's wind direction is a reference. For more accurate True Wind Angle (TWA) values, type the actual wind direction into the True Wind Direction input.
  • Each leg shows TWA as 42° Starboard or 42° Port.

3. Race the course

  • The Now panel shows the current leg, bearing and distance to the next mark, and which way you should round it.
  • Tap ✓ Mark rounded as you round each mark. Use ↶ Undo if you tap too soon. This allows you to keep track of what leg you are on, and what the next mark is.
  • 📍 Use GPS adds your live position to the chart, giving you distance and bearing from you to the next mark. Auto-off after 30 min.
  • 🧭 Compass overlays your heading (mobile only).
  • ⛶ opens the chart in fullscreen.

4. Other tabs

  • Race finder — pick a date and a class to get the course card, VHF channel, flag and the time of your warning signal.
  • Docs — Sailing Instructions, NoR, mark charts, course cards. Cached for offline use after first view.

Bearings are true; distances in nautical miles. Tap the ? button at the top any time to re-open this guide.

Use GPS for live navigation?

Turning on GPS lets the app show:

  • your boat's position on the chart;
  • live bearing & distance to the next mark;
  • a "Steer X° port/stbd" cue when paired with the compass.

Your location is used only on this device. It is never sent off your phone, never stored, and never shared with anyone — DBSC included. The app has no server and no analytics.

GPS will switch off automatically after 30 minutes to save your battery, or when you close the app. You can also turn it off any time by tapping the GPS button again.
